Approved copy, word for word

Everyone signs in with a phone number, a real photo and a social profile we check.

Nightlife spec rules for this step

  • Two loud moments only.
  • One-colour headlines in Big Shoulders Display.
  • Primary button is 56 high; hit targets are at least 44pt.
  • No in-app theme toggle. The screen follows the phone.

Welcome says plainly what we check. It does not say the word for a passed check.

    We take: Says the safety rules up front: public vetted venues, groups of 4 to 6, real names and photos, 18+. We say what we check on the first screen too.

    https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding, HTTP 200 from the build host, accessed 2026-10-27

B2.2Code entry with Change number, Send again and Paste.final/signin-code.html

Approved copy, word for word

We'll send a code on WhatsApp. It can be on another phone.

Nightlife spec rules for this step

  • Use the Field component for the phone number and the code.
  • Tabular figures on the phone number and the code.
  • One primary button per screen. Change number and Send again are secondary actions.
  • No SMS path. WhatsApp only, with one-tap autofill and a copy-code fallback.

Sign-in errors use the neutral Note, not danger.

    We leave: We send on WhatsApp only, never SMS. (Page blocks scripted fetches, read through a search result.)

Nightlife spec rules for this step

  • Tabs are never hidden or disabled.
  • Type scales with the phone settings. Tabular figures stay.
  • Reduced motion is respected everywhere. Motion exists in three places only.
  • Phosphor icons only. No blur or shadow on bars.

This step cites Apple HIG and Material 3 only. Where they differ, the platform wins for navigation and gesture, the Nightlife spec wins for colour, type and shape.
