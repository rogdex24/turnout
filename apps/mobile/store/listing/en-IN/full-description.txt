Turnout puts you in a small group of people who have tickets to the same show.

Pick a show you already have a ticket for. Turnout finds a few others going to it and puts you in a group. Women and non-binary people can choose a women-only group. Three hours before the show, your group sees each other's first names and photos, a cafe to meet at, and a chat. You meet at the cafe, then go in together.

How it works
- Browse the shows coming up near you.
- Say you are going and send a screenshot of your ticket.
- Turnout puts you in a group of four to six people with tickets to the same show.
- From three hours before, see your group, the meeting spot and the walking time.
- Tap "I'm here" when you arrive. Chat with the group, then enjoy the show.
- Afterwards, tell us if anyone made you uncomfortable. Only we see it.

What we ask for
- Your phone number. We send you a sign-in code on WhatsApp, so you need WhatsApp on that number.
- Your first name, your gender, which decides the groups you can join, and one photo of yourself.
- An Instagram or LinkedIn handle. A person on our team looks at it before you are put in a group. Your group never sees it.
- A screenshot of your ticket. A person on our team looks at it once, and we delete the file soon after the show.

Your privacy
- Nobody in your group sees your phone number or your handle.
- Your location stays on your phone. Turnout uses it only to show how far you are from the cafe.
- We do not sell your data and there is no advertising.
- Messages are deleted 30 days after the show.
- Usage data is off until you turn it on in Settings.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time, in Settings.

Turnout is not a dating app. It is a way to meet a few people who like the same show before you go in.

Questions or a complaint: the grievance officer's details are in Settings and in our privacy notice.
